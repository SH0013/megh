Your Google Drive, on your TV.

Megh turns the videos and photos in your Google Drive into a clean, simple library on Android TV and Fire TV. No server to set up, and nothing leaves your Drive except to your TV.

How it works

  1. Install Megh on your TV.
  2. On your phone or computer, go to sh0013.github.io/megh/link, enter the code shown on the TV, and sign in with Google.
  3. Your whole Drive appears on the TV, sorted into videos and photos by folder. Press play.

What you get

  • Plays almost any video format, with subtitles you can style and place below the picture.
  • Photos, including iPhone HEIC pictures.
  • A pitch black theme for OLED screens.
  • Open source, under the GPL-3.0 licence.
